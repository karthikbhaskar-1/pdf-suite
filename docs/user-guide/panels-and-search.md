# Panels and search

## Panels

Panels open on the left side of the window. A narrow strip of icons, called the rail, lets you open and close them.

- **Open or close a panel:** click its icon in the rail. Its header also has a close button.
- **Move a panel:** open the menu in the panel header and choose **Dock left** or **Dock right**.
- **Resize a panel:** drag its edge. It is 260 pixels wide by default, and can be from 180 to 480 pixels wide.
- **Too many panels:** the rail holds up to 10 icons. Extra icons go under the **…** button.

### Thumbnails

Thumbnails shows a small picture of each page.

- Click a thumbnail to jump to that page.
- Hold **Ctrl** or **Shift** and click to select more than one page.
- The current page has an accent-colored border.
- Thumbnails are never changed by page colors.
- To move the selection with the keyboard, press **Alt+Up** or **Alt+Down** (one page at a time), or **Alt+Home** and **Alt+End** (first and last page).
- Drag a thumbnail to a new place. A blue line shows where the page will go. A badge shows how many pages you are moving.
- A row of buttons under the header holds the most used page commands: Insert, Delete, Rotate, Extract and More. They act on the selected pages, or on the current page when nothing is selected.

For what each page command does, see [Organizing pages](organizing-pages.md).

### Bookmarks

Bookmarks shows the table of contents of the file, as a tree.

- Click a bookmark to jump to that page.
- Use **Left** and **Right** arrow keys to close and open a branch.

If the file has no bookmarks, the panel says so.

### Properties

Properties has two sections:

- **Document:** information about the whole file.
- **Current page:** information about the page you are on, including its size and rotation.

## Search

Search finds text in the open file.

- **Open the search bar:** **Ctrl+F**, or Edit ▸ **Find…**. The bar opens below the top bar.
- **Find the next match:** **F3**, or the next button.
- **Find the previous match:** **Shift+F3**, or the previous button.
- **Match case:** the **Aa** button.
- **Whole word:** the **whole word** button.

The bar shows "n of N", for example "3 of 12". All matches are highlighted. The current match is shown more strongly.

### Search limits

- On some government-style forms, the highlights can land in the wrong place, and text you type into form boxes is not searched.
